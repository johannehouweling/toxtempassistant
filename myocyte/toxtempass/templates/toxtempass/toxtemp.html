ToxTemp: the 77 questions for describing cell-based test methods

The ToxTemp template was developed by Krebs et al. It is not the work of the ToxTempAssistant team.

Krebs, A., Waldmann, T., Wilks, M. F., van Vugt-Lussenburg, B. M. A., van der Burg, B., et al. (2019). Template for the description of cell-based toxicological test methods to allow evaluation and regulatory use of the data. ALTEX, 36(4), 682–699. https://doi.org/10.14573/altex.1909271

The questions, notes and examples below are reproduced from that article and its supplementary material under the CC BY 4.0 license. We changed the formatting and left out notes that repeat the question. ToxTempAssistant is a separate tool that helps you draft answers to these questions.

ToxTemp turns the requirements of OECD Guidance Document 211 into guided questions, so that non-guideline, cell-based toxicological test methods are described completely and consistently. It has 70 questions in 11 sections, plus 7 sub-questions that structure the abstract.

Have protocols or publications for your assay? ToxTempAssistant can draft answers to these questions from your documents. See how it works.
