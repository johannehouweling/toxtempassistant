What joining a workspace means

  • Ownership does not change. Every investigation stays with its owner. As a member you can view and work on the investigations shared into the workspace. A ToxTemp you create inside someone else's investigation is credited to you as its creator, but you can open it only while that investigation is shared with you: if you leave, or the sharing ends, you lose access to it.
  • Reading from outside. Owners and admins of the workspace can create API tokens. A token lets an external server read, and only read, the investigations shared into the workspace: the questions, the answers and the ToxTemp as a PDF. {% if token_names %}This workspace has {{ token_names|length }} token{{ token_names|length|pluralize }} now: {{ token_names|join:", " }}.{% else %}This workspace has none now; you will get an email if one is created.{% endif %}
  • Credit. By joining you agree to be credited by name, with your organization and ORCID iD but never your email address, as an author of the ToxTemps you created or edited, in what those tokens read. The partners may use this when they publish. Without it you appear as “Contributor (not named)”. You can withdraw at any time, on the workspace in the user menu or by leaving it.
